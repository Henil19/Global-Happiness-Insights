# Executive summary

The supplied World Happiness Report 2025 workbook contains 147 unique country rows. Finland ranks first (7.736) and Afghanistan ranks last (1.364), a Ladder score gap of 6.372. India ranks 118 with score 4.389.

Strongest positive Pearson association: Social support (r=0.812, p=9.636e-36, n=147). No negative Pearson coefficients were observed among valid results. Weakest absolute Pearson association: Generosity (r=0.054, p=0.5132, n=147). These are country-level associations, not evidence of causation. The largest top-10 minus bottom-10 mean gap among the six indicators is GDP per capita (0.932).

The source contains 6 missing cells across 11 fields. Correlations use pairwise complete rows; group averages use available observations. No values were imputed and no outliers were dropped. Outliers are flagged with the 1.5×IQR rule for review. The workbook has no region or income category and no year-level observations, so regional comparisons and annual trends are unavailable. This new project analysis does not claim to reproduce the original internship analysis.
